  1. Days 1–9: AI intuition, Mac editor and terminal, Python, files and Git.
  2. Days 10–15: neural networks, tokens and local model calls with Ollama.
  3. Days 16–21: browser chat, testing, catch-up and an independent demo. Docker is optional.

Eight topic groups, roughly 25–35 hours of core work. Study for 1–2 hours at a time; at one hour a day, allow 25–35 study days. The slides include a course timetable near the start.

Before you begin

The guided track uses an Apple Silicon Mac with 24 GB of memory and teaches in English. No programming experience is assumed. Begin with the editor and terminal lessons; install Ollama when you reach the local-model section.
